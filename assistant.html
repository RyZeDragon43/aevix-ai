CONCEPT // ORBITAL COMPUTE
The infrastructure frontier

What if AI computing went to space?

Our long-term concept is to research space-based data centers as a possible path to future AI capacity.

  • NVIDIA: a potential technology relationship for AI chips and data-center computing.
  • SpaceX: a potential launch and space-logistics relationship for orbital infrastructure.
  • Tesla: a future aspiration to bring Aevix assistant experiences into vehicles, subject to technical and commercial agreements.

These are proposed partnership targets, not existing affiliations. Space data centers remain a research-stage concept with major challenges in cooling, radiation, communications, and cost.
