Strategic partnerships · Future vision

Building the future together.

Aevix plans to collaborate with established technology and aerospace companies to make affordable AI, large-scale computing, and eventually orbital data centers possible.

Proposed partnerships — not confirmed agreements
The companies we'd approach

Three partners. Three different strengths.

Each prospective partner would help solve a different part of Aevix's long-term strategy.

01 / Computing

NVIDIA

We would seek access to AI GPUs, accelerated computing platforms, networking, and technical expertise for efficient Earth-based AI data centers. Aevix would build its assistant and services on this computing foundation.

Proposed relationship: hardware procurement, technical collaboration, and infrastructure planning.

02 / Space launch

SpaceX

We would explore launch opportunities for small technology demonstrations, then potentially larger orbital computing modules. SpaceX could be a prospective launch provider; designing, operating, and cooling an orbital data center would remain Aevix's engineering responsibility with additional specialists.

Proposed relationship: launch services and mission integration.

03 / Vehicle experience

Tesla

We would propose an Aevix voice assistant experience for supported vehicles, focused on hands-free questions, planning, and convenience. Any vehicle integration would require Tesla's approval, compatible software interfaces, privacy safeguards, and driver-safety testing.

Proposed relationship: optional vehicle software integration.

How the plan fits together

From an AI app to an orbital network.

Launch Aevix AI

Build an affordable assistant for individuals and businesses; prove demand and improve the product.

Scale on Earth

Use advanced computing infrastructure, potentially powered by NVIDIA technology, to serve more customers.

Test in orbit

Study power, cooling, radiation shielding, communications, and economics with a small orbital demonstration launched through a future provider.

Why partners matter

Aevix focuses on the AI. Specialists help us scale.

Working with established companies could reduce the need to build every chip, rocket, and vehicle platform from scratch. However, partnerships are not guaranteed, and orbital computing remains an early-stage concept with major cost and engineering challenges.

The companies named on this page have not endorsed, sponsored, or agreed to partner with Aevix. This is a school business concept and strategic proposal.

Partnership proposal checklist
  • Demonstrate a working AI product and customer demand
  • Prepare a technical and financial business case
  • Identify specific hardware, launch, or software requirements
  • Contact appropriate business-development teams
  • Test security, safety, feasibility, and costs before launch
